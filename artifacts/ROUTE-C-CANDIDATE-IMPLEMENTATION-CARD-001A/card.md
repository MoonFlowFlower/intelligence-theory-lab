A future separately authorized Route C candidate may test a bounded
self-boundary / hidden-self-set inference proxy only if it beats both
`obs_only_family_max` and `fair_interventional_family_max` under predeclared

- `variance`

- greedy information-gain policy.

- `fsm_planner`.
- `episodic_traversal`.

`fair_interventional_family_max` must be computed as max over the full family.
All must enter the saturation judgment.

## 9. Candidate-Vs-Baseline And Saturation Gates

Future candidate must beat both `obs_only_family_max` and
`fair_interventional_family_max` under predeclared, hashed margin values. If
strongest fair interventional baseline equals, exceeds, or approaches candidate
within frozen margin, the verdict is `close_or_downgrade`.

Both-scored-high is not success.

## 10. RF-1 Through RF-4

### RF-1: Forged-Provenance Option-2 Closure

Future implementation must include a co-forged-input positive control where
value, basis, and recorded inputs are self-consistent. Recorded inputs must be
pinned to independent source artifact hashes. Every material row must either
re-execute the producer on source-pinned inputs or rederive basis from
source-pinned inputs and prove those inputs were not co-forged. Self-declared
input/basis consistency is insufficient. Candidate-run material rows are in
scope.

### RF-2: Pre-Run Mechanism-Component Lock

The mechanism component under test must be minimal, independently specified
outside candidate source, and hashed before run. Candidate advantage cannot
define it after the fact. Feature-impoverishment control must use this
pre-hashed component. If granting it to the fair baseline does not allow that
baseline to approach candidate performance, the baseline is unfair/impoverished
and the run is invalid.

### RF-3: Independent Anchoring Of B1-B5 / N1-N4

Future implementation must independently bind B1 F-forge closure, B2 Phase 0
passive-family superset, B3 fair-interventional baseline hardening, B4
margin/saturation failability, B5 six graph-cache challengers, N1 prior
negatives, N2 frozen margin predeclaration/hash, N3 replay tautology exclusion,
and N4 truth-seed isolation.

### RF-4: Future Implementation Diff Hygiene

Future implementation must produce git-verifiable diff evidence, exact
changed-file proof, and separate proof of whether `src/`, `tests/`, `scripts/`,
Gate, mainline, runtime, scheduler, bridge, product/admission, credential, or
push-script paths were touched. Push, tag, and remote anchor remain forbidden
while PAT hygiene is unresolved.

## 11. Ablation Plan

Future implementation must run real rerun ablations, not report edits:

- remove mechanism-specific state.
- remove interventional access.
- remove update path.
- remove replay state.
- remove candidate memory/cache.
- remove self-boundary-specific feature.
- shuffle target labels.
- swap twin pairs where passive distribution is held constant.
- remove candidate-only fields.
- replace candidate policy with strongest fair baseline policy.

## 12. Trace / Replay Plan

Replay must recompute candidate behavior from serialized state plus legal
observation/action history. Stored-hash comparison alone is insufficient.
Tautological replay controls do not count toward failability.

- value-level passive leakage.
- schema/name/key leakage.
- order leakage.
- action/API leakage.
- serialized-state leakage.
- result-json leakage.
- candidate-only-field leakage.
- train/heldout contamination.
- truth-seed contamination.

## 14. Computed-Evidence Provenance Gate

No mechanism score may come from literals, static dictionaries, self-report,
unconditional clean reports, or hand-filled JSON.

Every material score must include `producer_function`, `source_path`,
`recorded_inputs`, `source_pinned_input_artifact_hash`, `run_id`, `seed`,
`context_id`, `episode_id`, `aggregation`, `code_path_hash`,
`recompute_command`, and `output_artifact`.

Material rows include candidate score, passive baseline scores,
`obs_only_family_max`, fair interventional baseline scores, graph-cache
challenger scores, margin decision, saturation decision, ablation scores, replay
recomputation, leakage scan, leakage positive controls, forged-provenance
controls, and final verdict selection.
