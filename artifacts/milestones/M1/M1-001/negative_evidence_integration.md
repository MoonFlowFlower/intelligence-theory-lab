# M1-001 Negative Evidence Integration

The VCCO / VCAC / FOPC lineage remains binding negative evidence for any LCC successor. The milestone review treats those failures as constraints, not as history to overwrite.

## Closed Or Downgraded Claims

| Lineage | Result | Constraint For LCC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full VCCO | Full six-component necessity claim closed. | Do not infer component necessity from aggregate pass metrics. |
| Plastic selection | No-op / unsupported under current implementation. | No no-op ablation may count as mechanism evidence. |
| Repertoire expansion | No-op / unsupported under current implementation. | No trace label or macro may count as causal component without action-distribution effect. |
| VCAC-Core | Boundary and action-closure failed control-loop causality. | Trace presence and replay separability are not control-loop causality. |
| Viability/compression closeout | Score sensitivity survived more than action-distribution causality. | Score-only influence is not enough. |
| FOPC | Future-action-variety proxy was action-causal but reducible to action labels. | No future-option proxy may reuse action labels, evaluator metrics, or static safety tables. |

## Hard Constraints

- No action-label future-option proxy.
- No trace-only boundary or control claim.
- No score-only causality claim.
- No no-op ablation claim.
- No evaluator metric feature reuse.
- No hidden future, oracle transition table, or plan table.
- No scenario ID, task ID, cycle ID, contract ID, object name, entity ID, semantic action label, or semantic goal label shortcut.
- No theory rename without stronger kill tests.

## How LCC Addressed These Constraints

- Cycle 000 and Cycle 001 directly attacked action-label shortcut through label permutation and effect-swap tests.
- Cycle 002 attacked passive-correlation shortcuts through own-intervention evidence.
- Cycle 003 required diagnostic action selection under causal ambiguity.
- Cycle 004-008 extended the same action-distribution causal requirement across sequential, nonstationary, representation, relational, and goal-conditioned contracts.
- Cycle 009 attacked per-cycle specialization.
- Cycle 010 attacked contract overfitting through candidate freeze, blind holdout, independent scoring, generic baselines, replication, and negative controls.

## Remaining Risk

LCC may still be an operational contract suite for model-based causal control rather than an independent theory. This is not a failure of the bounded evidence, but it blocks any upgrade to theory support.
