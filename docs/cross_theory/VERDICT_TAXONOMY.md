# Verdict Taxonomy

The future tournament must not be structured to force an LCC win. Collapse into an existing theory is a valid and useful result.

## Allowed Future Execution Verdicts

- `lcc_distinct_bounded_advantage`
- `lcc_collapses_into_model_based_rl`
- `lcc_collapses_into_causal_model_based_control`
- `lcc_collapses_into_active_inference`
- `lcc_collapses_into_empowerment`
- `lcc_equivalent_to_strong_heuristic`
- `lcc_loses_to_generic_baseline`
- `all_theories_fail_negative_controls`
- `tournament_inconclusive_revise_contract`

## Meaning Of Collapse

Collapse means a strong non-oracle competitor reproduces the LCC pass profile under the same anti-shortcut constraints, within the equivalence band, without forbidden information, and with stable replication.

Collapse is not a bad result. It means LCC may be better treated as an operational evidence discipline, redteam protocol, or special case of that theory family.

## Meaning Of Distinct Bounded Advantage

`lcc_distinct_bounded_advantage` means LCC has a bounded operational advantage over all valid non-oracle competitors under the predeclared tournament contract. It still does not prove LCC theory support, a bottom intelligence principle, AGI, consciousness, self-awareness, life, EGO readiness, or robust universal support.

## Inconclusive Conditions

Use `tournament_inconclusive_revise_contract` if:

- Multiple competitors fail due to ambiguous task definitions.
- Metrics cannot distinguish pass-profile equivalence.
- Negative controls or leak scans are incomplete.
- Baseline implementations are materially weaker than the competitor cards require.
- Statistical replication is unstable.

## Not Allowed

- A verdict that treats oracle upper bound as a valid competitor.
- A verdict that upgrades LCC to theory support.
- A verdict that authorizes a general LCC agent or EGO migration.
- A verdict that hides LCC collapse behind ambiguous wording.
