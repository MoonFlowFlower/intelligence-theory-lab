## Strongest Objection

The strongest objection is that this policy could become another governance-only loop. A discovery/admission split only helps if it changes routing behavior. Therefore the route policy explicitly closes same-family repair after cheap-baseline closure, requires at least three candidate families before selecting a new Gate4 replacement design, and classifies report-only or anchor-only outputs as governance-only unless they preserve negative evidence or change a downstream route.

## Discovery Vs Admission Policy

Discovery mode is for comparing mechanism families before admission-grade evidence. It has lower documentation burden, no mainline claims, no remote anchor unless a route boundary is created, no same-family repair after cheap-baseline closure, cheap-baseline-first preflight, and a minimum of three candidate mechanism families before selecting a new Gate4 replacement design.

Admission mode is only for candidates that survive discovery. It requires computed-evidence provenance, independent callable baselines, real ablations, positive-control leakage scanners, replay recomputation from serialized state plus observation where applicable, and bounded claims only when computed evidence supports them.
