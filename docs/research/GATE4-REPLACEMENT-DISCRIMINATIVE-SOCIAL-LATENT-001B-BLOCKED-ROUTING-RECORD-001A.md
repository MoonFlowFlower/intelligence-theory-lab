## Why This Is A Blocker Rather Than A Pass

001B cannot be routed as positive replacement-Gate4 evidence because the
independent audit found that the harness's observation surface contains a
deterministic, invertible encoding of the target. The intended discriminative
claim was that candidate success required active information acquisition and
feedback-dependent state update against dangerous independent baselines. That
claim is not supported when an unmandated observation-only baseline can recover
the answer from an allowed observation feature.

The exact blocking evidence preserved from the audit is:

- `baseline_hint` deterministically encodes target as target + 1 cyclic shift.
- An unmandated observation-only `hint_inverter` baseline scores `1.0`.
- The mandated strongest ordinary baseline, `transition_table`, scores
  `0.2857142857142857`.
- The candidate scores `1.0`.

This means the candidate's reported advantage over the mandated baseline suite
is partially explained by observation-target leakage plus a missing baseline,
not only by the intended active-query and feedback-update mechanism.

## Why This Is Not Fabrication

The audit classifies the 001B evidence as computed and reproducible. The audit

candidate, baseline, ablation, threshold, margin, replay, and split-coverage
results are produced by callable code paths. The block is therefore a
