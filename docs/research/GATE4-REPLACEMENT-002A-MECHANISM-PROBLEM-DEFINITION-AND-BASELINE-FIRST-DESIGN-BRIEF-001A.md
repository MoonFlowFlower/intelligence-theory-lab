## Strongest Baseline Explanation

The strongest baseline explanation for any future success is still non-mechanism recovery: pair-count tables, trace lookup, partner-ID lookup, preference tables, full-bundle decoding, serialized-state decoding, finite-state policy, query-capable imitation, belief tables, graph/cache controls, or a non-social world model.

Therefore a future Gate4 cannot define candidate success first and add baselines later. It must define faithful callable baselines, access rights, and collapse conditions before candidate metrics.

## Strongest Objection

Any closed toy task with compact target structure can collapse again. If partner changes, hidden preferences, answer labels, context IDs, fixture paths, serialized state, observation tokens, or trace records encode the target, then a future pass would again measure recovery from encoding rather than social-latent mechanism.

## Better Mechanism Framing

The better future framing is:

```text
Under partial observability and partner changes, can the agent maintain and update separate self/other latent state from observed behavior, use that state to predict and act under changed conditions, and degrade under real interventions on the social-latent update path in ways fair baselines cannot match?
```

This uses the interpretability reframe in `docs/EVIDENCE-GATE-INTERPRETABILITY-REFRAME-001A.md`: complete human semantic interpretation of every latent component is not the gate. Auditability, trace/replay, ablation sensitivity, counterfactual intervention sensitivity, baseline non-equivalence, leakage resistance, and claim-ceiling enforcement are the gate.

## Rejected Routes

Rejected or not recommended as the next route:

- 001D-derived static puzzle variants: closed by 001E/001F because they risk target recovery through faithful encodings.
- predictive world-model-only route: useful as a component or baseline, but not sufficient if social-latent state and partner-intervention gates are not primary.
- active-inference/self-other belief route: mechanism-relevant, but too abstract before a concrete baseline-resistant environment exists.
- causal-intervention-only route: strong evidence discipline, but too narrow as a full problem definition without dynamic partner-belief scaffolding.
- defer Gate4 and return to cross-gate theory coverage: valid fallback if the recommended route cannot be specified without decoder collapse.

## Minimum Future Evidence Contract

The future executable design must require:

- heldout partner dynamics, not only heldout labels;
- partner policy shifts after interaction history;
- counterfactual partner response tests;
- intervention on candidate social-latent state;
- intervention on partner-observation stream;
- train, heldout, and counterfactual context IDs with leakage audit;
- positive-control leakage case;
- negative-control no-signal case;
- replay from serialized state plus observation;
- independent callable baselines with fair access;
- real ablation reruns under intervention.
