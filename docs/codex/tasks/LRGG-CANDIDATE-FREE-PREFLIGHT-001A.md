### R2: Define Headroom Honestly

Candidate-free headroom is defined exactly as:

```text
budget_faithful_visible_channel_oracle - max(fair_panel_including_amortized_meta_learner)
```

Within R2 shorthand, `oracle` means `budget_faithful_visible_channel_oracle` only.

`max(fair_panel_including_amortized_meta_learner)` includes mandatory degenerate controls /
trivial predictors: `predict_all`, `predict_none`, and `constant_k_sweep`.

Candidate-free headroom is inadmissible unless degenerate controls are inside the fair-panel max.
If the apparent gap disappears when trivial predictors are included, verdict =
`rejected_metric_degenerate` or `rejected_baseline_saturated` as appropriate.

Do not define headroom as:

- oracle minus weak baseline;
- interventional minus observational;
- forbidden interventional-minus-observational gap;
- candidate minus cheap baseline;
- Rung3-like apparent headroom when strong learners fail capability floor.

Equal-access oracle/fair-panel rule:

```text
The oracle and the fair panel must have equal legal access to observations, actions,
interventions, queries, and do(.) budget. If the oracle has do(.) or query access denied to the
fair panel, then `oracle - max(fair_panel)` is classified as forbidden
interventional-minus-observational gap, not candidate-free headroom.
```

Allowed oracle access:

```text
legal observations + legal actions + same frozen intervention/query/do(.) budget available to
the full fair panel, including the fitted cross-episode amortized / ideal-observer learner.
```

Forbidden oracle access:

```text
direct hidden_rule_graph reads;
direct remapping reads;
direct latent-value reads;
generator-truth imports;
scorer-truth imports;
candidate-circular logic;
any intervention/query/do(.) budget not equally available to the fair panel.
```

Headroom must be budget-robust.

Mandatory budget robustness protocol:

```text
budget_robustness_protocol:
  - pre-register at least three fair-panel budget levels or a canonical budget sweep design;
  - compute headroom at each budget level;
  - aggregate with pre-registered lower-confidence-bound rule;
  - candidate-free headroom is admissible only if
    `oracle - max(fair_panel_including_amortized_meta_learner)` remains >=
    `candidate_free_headroom_delta` at the required LCB under the frozen budget robustness rule;
  - if gap shrinks below threshold as fair budget increases, verdict =
    `rejected_baseline_saturated` or no-headroom negative;
  - if strong fair learners fail debug/ID floor, verdict = `INVALID`, not headroom.
```

All budget levels, aggregation rules, confidence rules, and threshold bands must remain
`OPERATOR_FREEZE_BEFORE_RESULTS` until operator freeze and must not be selected after results.
