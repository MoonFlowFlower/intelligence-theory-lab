Control provenance is frozen as `control_comparison_reason`,
`frozen_control_panel_id`, `control_contrast_ids`, `control_signed_margin`,
`control_confidence_interval`, `control_equivalence_band`, and
`control_equivalence_type`. Rival provenance uses the corresponding rival

`ADMISSION_CEILING_SATURATION`, `OWN_RULE_AMORTIZED_PARITY`, `CONTROL_BETTER`,

Semantics are immutable:

- Only a valid causal ablation failure produces `evidence_state=ABSENT`.
- A passing causal signature plus shortcut-control parity produces
  `PRESENT_BOUNDED + CONTROL_EQUIVALENT`.

  not-run, saturated, or separated.
- A strong negative may satisfy a falsifier by driving advantage to or below
  parity, but it is not statistical equivalence without the frozen powered
  equivalence test.

  equivalence blocks an architectural-specialness claim.

## 5. Frozen arm roles and component mapping

Every future H0 arm, including contrast-specific reference/full-arm instances,
must be assigned ex ante to exactly one of `CAUSAL_ABLATION`,
`SHORTCUT_CONTROL`, `RIVAL`, or `INTEGRITY_CONTROL`. A single arm id cannot carry
multiple roles. Full/reference instances inherit the contrast class and must be
distinctly identified when the same executable is reused across classes.

The contract freezes all named panels and the machine mapping fields
`component_id`, `evidence_contrast_ids`, `control_comparison_ids`,
`rival_comparison_ids`, `integrity_dependency_ids`, and `claim_template_ids`.
The mandatory graph/cache family is expanded into concrete ids, not represented
by an unauditable prose alias.

The required mappings are:

- `V_model` causal: planner bypass, checkpoint swap, prediction
  counterfactual. Shortcut: observation-only, window history, exact lookup,
  nearest neighbor, graph lookup, transition table, successor map, count table,
  FSM planner, episodic traversal, and candidate-own-rule amortized when
  applicable.
- `V_online` causal: no update and shuffled outcome. Shortcut:
  candidate-own-rule batched/amortized and same-feedback-budget table/history
  learner.
- `V_replay` causal: matched replay off. Integrity: corrupted replay detector.
  Shortcut: window history, episodic traversal, the expanded graph/cache family,
  and matched no-replay amortized learner.
- `V_memory` causal: memory read off, memory zero, source deletion, and history
  replacement. Shortcut: recency, summary, RAG, exact/nearest lookup, window
  history, and the expanded graph/cache family.
- `V_transfer` causal/carrier: fresh init, from scratch, checkpoint only, replay
  reset, memory reset, and full carryover. Shortcut: equal-budget persistent
  history/cache/batched carriers.
- `V_special`: the frozen rival panel is the comparative proposition; the full
  shortcut panel is a claim-admissibility qualifier. Its causal mapping is the
  preregistered union of the component causal contrasts whose capabilities the
  specialness claim references.

`corrupted_replay_detector` and `matched_replay_off` are distinct arms, roles,
and contrast ids. They may never be merged.

## 9. Frozen integrity blast radius

Future integrity gates contain `integrity_gate_id`, `gate_class`,
`affected_arms`, `affected_estimands`, `affected_components`, `failure_action`,
`state_mapping`, and `resolver_function`.

1. Shared replay/recompute-engine failure invalidates every component that
   depends on that engine and may be global when the dependency matrix says so.
2. One-arm/trace recomputation failure invalidates only dependent estimands and
   components.
3. `matched_replay_off` tying full maps `V_replay=ABSENT`; it is not an
   instrument failure.
4. A matched no-replay shortcut learner tying maps
   `control_comparison_state=CONTROL_EQUIVALENT`.
5. A replay-reset carrier tie affects only the mapped `V_transfer`/replay-carrier
   proposition.

Provenance, access, and global leakage are normally run-global. Local component
leakage propagates only through the frozen dependency matrix and never through
post-result discretion. Unconditional propagation of every replay failure to
every component is forbidden.
