## Problem Definition

Build a small machine-checkable preflight guard that blocks successor task
cards when they omit known negative evidence or convert bounded audit language
into stronger authorization.

The task is not to research a new theory, continue investigating Fable, rerun
experiments, repair old artifacts, normalize 001C, or refreeze evidence.

## Rules

A successor task fails admission if it:

1. cites `representational_gap_preflight_bounded_pass` or 001A pass without
   citing `REPRESENTATIONAL-GAP-PREFLIGHT-001A-AUDIT-CLOSEOUT` or explicit
   001A supersession;
2. cites a representational-gap successor without citing
   `representational_gap_001b_failed_count_or_statistic_control_solved`;
3. cites current `PREDICTIVE-ACTION-LEARNING-CONTRACT-001C-CLOSEOUT.md`
   without citing `PREDICTIVE-ACTION-LEARNING-CONTRACT-001C-CANONICAL-ERRATA-001A`;
4. treats `process_intervention_001a_independent_audit_pass_with_caveats` as
   executable authorization;
5. treats verdict-string tests as acceptance evidence;
6. treats `replay_verification_pass` as sufficient while omitting
   `gate1_preflight_failed_graph_cache_collapse`;
7. attributes causality to Fable without provenance, diff, and mechanism
   evidence.

## Acceptance Gate

The gate must produce machine-readable pass/fail results for:

- a bad successor task that fails all seven rules;
- a properly cited successor task that passes;
- a result artifact recording both evaluations.

## Claim Ceiling

Maximum allowed claim:

```text
bounded successor-task admission guard against known false-confidence risks
```

This does not prove Fable-caused data poisoning, no poisoning, 001C validity,
001C invalidity, Gate 1 evidence, same-agent bridge evidence, companion
readiness, EGO readiness, agency, consciousness, subjective experience, real
emotion, self-awareness, functional-subject status, electronic life, or AGI.

## Stop Conditions

Stop if any implementation step:

- modifies old artifacts;
- rewrites protected 001C files;
- reruns experiments;
- introduces a new theory;
- attributes Fable causality without provenance, diff, and mechanism evidence;
- treats this guard as mechanism evidence.
